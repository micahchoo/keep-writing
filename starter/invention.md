Prompts. Every other register names a way of retrieving a life already
lived; a prompt calls for the opposite act, and the answer does not exist
until it is written.

They are scored on their own page (`PROMPT-RUBRIC.md`), which contradicts
the Bank test on purpose: a Bank question must be unguessable about ONE
life, a prompt must be open to many readings. A question that scores well
on both is rare and is probably neither. They speak as *we* where a Bank
question speaks as *you*, so the voice tells you which you have been
handed.
