Questions about the life that happens between projects: familiar places,
small encounters, things you handle, and whatever holds your attention.

These use the Bank test: only you can supply the particulars. Each question
arrives on its own, asks one thing, and leaves the answer's meaning to you.
A short answer can be complete; a small occasion can open into a story.

The headings group subjects. The register on each question still names the
kind of answer it asks for.
